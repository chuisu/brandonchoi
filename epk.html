bran choi

is a believer in human beings. A believer in the creative soul and capacity for motion that humanity has throughout its short tenure on this earth proven. Inspired by black music, American folk music, and traditional Korean music, bran's music underpins this creative spirit, and is written and performed with the awareness of what it is to be a human being on the planet right-fucking-now. Thanks for reading this, fellow suffering being. Here we are.

bran has performed at the Jazz Gallery, Birdland, Dizzy's Club Coca Cola, among many other various and illustrious venues in NYC, averaging ~50 heads in rooms like Threes Brewing (Gowanus) and the Jazz Gallery (NoMad), and ~100 heads in rooms like the Red Pavilion (Bushwick). bran has performed for crowds of ~2500 at places like the Granada Theater in Santa Barbara, CA, and a crowd of ~400 at the Brooklyn Steel.
